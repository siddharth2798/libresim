# libresim logo options

Eight restrained alternatives to the current rainbow logo (flat colours, one or two hues plus neutrals).
Each folder has `mark.svg`, `logo.svg` (horizontal lockup with the tagline), `logo_light.png`, `logo_dark.png`
and `mark.png` (512). `contact_sheet.png` compares all eight; `contact_sheet_variants.png` shows light and dark.
Regenerate with `tools/.venv/bin/python tools/branding/make_logo_options.py` (SVG text is outlined, no fonts needed).

| # | Name | Concept | Colours | Font (licence) |
|---|------|---------|---------|----------------|
| 01 | Badge (`01-badge`) | High-wing C172 climbing out in a round badge, horizon tint below | navy, white, orange cheat-line | Open Sans (Apache-2.0) SemiBold |
| 02 | Wing-l (`02-wing-l`) | Top-view light aircraft whose fuselage is the letter l; chevron wings | navy, sky blue | Fira Sans (SIL OFL 1.1) SemiBold |
| 03 | Climb-out (`03-climb-out`) | Two horizon lines with a light aircraft rotating away above them | charcoal, aviation orange | Open Sans (Apache-2.0) Regular |
| 04 | Ghats (`04-ghats`) | Flat-topped Sahyadri ridges with an aircraft above, round badge | deep green, cream | Roboto Slab (Apache-2.0) Bold |
| 05 | Runway 09 (`05-runway-09`) | Runway designator 09 over threshold piano keys (VABB 09/27) | asphalt black, white, amber | Fira Sans (SIL OFL 1.1) Medium |
| 06 | Monogram ls (`06-monogram`) | Heavy ls monogram, the l cut at a climb angle, rounded tile | teal, white | Open Sans (Apache-2.0) ExtraBold |
| 07 | Plane dot (`07-plane-dot`) | Wordmark only: a small aircraft replaces the dot of the i | ink, signal red | Fira Sans (SIL OFL 1.1) Medium |
| 08 | Paper plane (`08-paper-plane`) | Folded paper plane at the end of a dotted flight path | indigo tints | Open Sans (Apache-2.0) Bold |

Notes: `mark.png` is transparent for badge/tile marks and sits on a solid background for open glyph marks
(02, 03, 08). Option 07's lockup is the wordmark alone (plane for the dot of the i); its mark is the i with the plane.

The tagline in every lockup is set in Open Sans Regular (Apache-2.0) or Fira Sans Regular (SIL OFL 1.1),
matching the wordmark family.
