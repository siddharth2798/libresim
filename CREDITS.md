free-sim is currently for personal, local use. Read docs/FLIGHTGEAR_NOTES.md before distributing anything.

## In use
- **Flight model data:** JSBSim `aircraft/c172p/c172p.xml`, `engine/engIO360C.xml`, `engine/prop_75in2f.xml`, from the JSBSim Team (https://jsbsim.org), LGPL-2.1. Converted to JSON and tuned by free-sim (tools/06_jsbsim_to_json.py).
- **Imagery:** Sentinel-2 cloudless, https://s2maps.eu, by EOX IT Services GmbH (contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data 2024), **CC BY-NC-SA 4.0**, non-commercial. The derived imagery tiles inherit this licence.
- **Elevation:** Copernicus GLO-30 DEM © DLR e.V. 2010–2014 and © Airbus Defence and Space GmbH 2014–2018, provided under COPERNICUS by the European Union and ESA.
- **Map data:** © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0 (https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright). Runways, taxiways, roads and buildings are derived from OSM.
- **Airports & navaids:** OurAirports (https://ourairports.com), public domain.
